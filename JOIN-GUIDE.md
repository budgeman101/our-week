# 🏡 Welcome to Our Week + Our Kitchen

*(Ben: replace `YOUR-FAMILY-CODE` with the code you made for them before
sending this — don't commit their real code to the repo, it's public.)*

A shared weekly planner and kitchen app for your family — a day-at-a-glance
plan, a self-sorting to-do list, projects, shared notes, and a full kitchen
side (shopping list, pantry, meals, receipt scanning). Free, no accounts, no
ads. It syncs live between your phones.

## Your family's code

> **`YOUR-FAMILY-CODE`**

This one code is your key to **everything** — the planner, the kitchen, your
reminders, and the AI scanner are all tied to it.

- Everyone in your home types the **same code**, once, on each phone.
- **Keep it private.** The code is the only lock on your family's data —
  treat it like a house key, not a username.
- **Don't change it without asking Ben first.** Reminders and the receipt
  scanner are registered to this exact code; if it changes, they quietly stop
  until Ben re-points them.

## Getting set up (2 minutes per phone)

1. Open **https://budgeman101.github.io/our-week/** on your phone.
2. At the welcome card: type your family code, then your **two names** — put
   the partner whose **work shifts** you plan around second (their shifts get
   their own spot on each day, and the app plans around their work nights).
3. Tap whichever name is *you*, then **Start**.
4. Make it a real app — it needs this for reminders too:
   - **iPhone (Safari):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
   - **Android (Chrome):** Menu → **Add to Home screen / Install app**.
5. For the kitchen side: tap **Kitchen** in the purple header, and type the
   **same code** at its welcome card.

Repeat on the second phone with the same code — you now share one live plan.

## Good to know

- **Tap anything to edit it.** Every line can be reworded, re-assigned
  (tap the name chip), split in two, or deleted. **+ Add** is everywhere.
- **Type tasks in plain words** — "mow the lawn on Thursday" sorts itself to
  the right person, area, and day. Using a name ("groceries with Sarah")
  assigns it to them.
- **Reminders** (shift and appointment nudges) — turn them on in ⚙ Settings
  on each phone once the app is on your Home Screen.
- **The AI scanner** (snap a receipt or a handwritten list, import a recipe
  from a link) runs on a small paid service that **Ben covers** — your family
  gets **30 scans a month**, which is plenty for weekly shops. The app tells
  you when you're running low; if you need more one month, just ask him.
  Everything else is unlimited and free.
- **Where your stuff lives:** your family's data sits in the same free Google
  database Ben's family uses, in your own private space only your code can
  open. Ben hosts it — he doesn't look at it, but as the host he technically
  could, and he's the one to call if anything acts up.

That's it — pick a day and start planning your week.
